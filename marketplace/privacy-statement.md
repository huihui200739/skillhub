# Privacy notice

Welcome to Team Skills Hub. Please read the following before you use this site.

## 1. Information we process

- When you sign in through a third-party platform, we receive basic account details needed for authentication (such as user id, display name, and avatar) to show your session, attribute published content, and support security reviews.
- When you browse, search, or download plugins or skill bundles, our servers may record minimal access logs (such as time, API path, and resource identifiers) for reliability and aggregated statistics.

## 2. Retention and security

- We keep this information only as long as needed to provide the service and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.
- We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to this site, except where required by law or with your clear consent.

## 3. Third-party sites

This site may link to third-party websites. Those sites are responsible for their own privacy practices.

## 4. Contact

If you have questions about this notice, contact your organization administrator or technical support.
